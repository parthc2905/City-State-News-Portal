Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?
+
Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your access will continue until the end of your current billing period, and you won't be charged again.
What payment methods do you accept?
+
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI, net banking, and mobile wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay). All payments are processed securely through Razorpay.
Is there a free trial?
+
We offer a 14-day free trial for new subscribers. No credit card required! You can explore all premium features before committing to a paid plan.
Can I share my subscription?
+
Individual subscriptions are for personal use only. However, you can access your account on up to 3 devices. We also offer family plans for up to 5 members at a discounted rate.
